# Chulapa cover style

These are the original covers used as the visual basis for this skill. Their shared qualities are realistic textures, window light, deliberate composition and a distinct subject that survives the header crop. Vary the color temperature and props to suit the subject.

- [Chulapa v2.2.0](chulapa-v2-2-0.jpg) uses a flowchart and Madrid prints with a warm-to-cool lighting transition.
- [Chulapa v2.1.0](chulapa-v2-1-0.jpg) uses page proofs, color swatches and a magnifying glass in an overhead scene.
- [Ask Chulapa](ask-chulapa.jpg) uses a laptop and a paper reference guide in cool daylight.
- [Chulapa v2.3.0 revised cover](chulapa-v2-3-0-v2.jpg) puts notebooks and cards in the middle of the frame. The earlier version reserved space for overlaid text, which left too little subject matter in the wide header crop.

For a Madrid reference, start with the architecture prints in v2.2.0 or the balcony and stone façades in the revised v2.3.0 cover. Pick a clue that suits the new article, such as a Metro photograph, a wrought-iron balcony, street architecture or a traditional fan.

## Prompts used

### v2.2.0

> Create a wide photorealistic editorial still life for the Chulapa v2.2.0 release. A walnut desk lit by warm daylight from one side and cool evening shadow on the other, with an unlettered flowchart and three small prints of Madrid architecture arranged across the middle. Muted indigo, cream and aged wood. Landscape near 1.91:1. Keep important details visible in a shallow horizontal crop through the center. No words, numbers, people, logos or watermark.

### v2.1.0

> Create a wide photorealistic editorial photograph for the Chulapa v2.1.0 release about site skins, metadata and search. Overhead cream magazine page proofs with abstract gray typesetting blocks, muted terracotta, sage and ink-blue color swatches, and a small glass magnifier on a wooden editing table. Soft indirect light and realistic paper texture. Landscape near 1.91:1. Keep the magnifier and swatches in the shallow horizontal center crop for the blog header. No readable type, people, branding or watermark.

### Ask Chulapa

> Create a wide photorealistic editorial photograph for the Ask Chulapa documentation assistant article. A compact matte laptop beside an open paper reference guide and cream index cards with colored tabs, on a deep blue-gray study desk in soft daylight. Its screen shows simple generic conversation shapes and source cards without lettering. Natural textures in muted blue, ivory and warm wood. Landscape near 1.91:1. Keep the laptop and printed guide recognizable in a shallow horizontal center crop. No people, robots, readable text, logos or watermark.

### v2.3.0 revision

> Revise the attached Chulapa v2.3.0 editorial cover. Preserve the warm Madrid study, walnut desk, cream notebooks, colored chapter tabs, blank cards, pencil and natural photograph-like texture. Recompose the still life so the tabbed notebook edges and a few blank cards cross the horizontal middle and carry the image within a shallow center crop. The blog title appears below the banner, so fill the central band with the subjects instead of leaving space for typography. Retain the soft window light and muted rust, ochre and brown palette, with the window further in the background. Wide landscape near 1.91:1. No lettering, numbers, people, logos or watermark.

---
name: skin-migration
description: Migrate and verify Chulapa skins and the shared components they style in chulapa2. Use for porting a skin from Chulapa, checking its palette identity, or auditing skin contrast at mobile and desktop widths.
---

Before any other step, read and follow
`.github/agents/skin-migration.agent.md` from the repository root. That file is
the single source of truth for this agent's procedure. Read every guide it
links to, and follow the project rules in `AGENTS.md`.

When you finish, report the skins and files you changed, the tests you ran with
their results, any measured contrast exceptions, and the work that remains
open.
